Problem Statement
›AQI platforms rely on fixed stations — no localized community-level prediction exists
›Citizens have no tool to estimate air quality directly from sky images
›Cloudburst & flood detection inaccessible to ordinary citizens without equipment
✓
Proposed Solution
EcoAlert is an AI-driven platform that predicts AQI using meteorological data & sky images, detects cloudbursts and floods via LSTM & XGBoost models, and delivers real-time push alerts to mobile and web users across Pakistan — sourcing live data from NASA GPM and PMD.
