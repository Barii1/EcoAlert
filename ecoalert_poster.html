Problem Statement
AQI platforms rely on fixed stations — no localized prediction exists for communities.
The public has no accessible tool to estimate air quality from sky images.
Cloudburst detection remains inaccessible to ordinary citizens without specialized equipment.
Proposed Solution

EcoAlert is an AI-driven platform that predicts Air Quality Index using meteorological data and sky images, detects cloudbursts using machine learning, and delivers real-time alerts to mobile and web users — making environmental intelligence accessible to all citizens.
